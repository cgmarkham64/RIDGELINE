Field Notes
Up before first light to catch the alpenglow on the Maroon Bells from the lake. Not a breath of wind — glass water, perfect reflection. Hit West Maroon Pass by 9am before afternoon convection built. Hasley Basin completely empty. Peak wildflower bloom everywhere. Best composition of the trip: looking northeast toward the Bells with Indian paintbrush in the foreground. Tripod earned its weight today.
